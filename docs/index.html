X Goals

Your X analytics, on the toolbar: daily replies you post, timeline posts, verified followers, and Original Content Rewards impressions vs the 500K bar.

Download (.zip) View source

Free & open source (MIT). Your data stays in your browser — nothing is uploaded.

Install in Chrome

  1. Download the .zip above and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Click Load unpacked and select the unzipped folder that contains manifest.json.
  4. Stay logged in to X in that Chrome profile, then click the toolbar icon → Refresh.

Chrome only allows one-click installs from the Chrome Web Store, so outside the store this manual 30-second setup is required. The extension polls x.com/i/account_analytics every 5 minutes (configurable) and shows your counts on the toolbar icon and popup.

What it tracks

Replies you posted — goal 60/dayOutbound replies from the Posts/Replies chart or ReplyCreate counts. The inbound “Replies” card is shown only as a note.
Timeline posts — goal 3/dayOriginal posts you publish (TweetCreate + QuoteCreate), with its own daily goal.
Verified followers — goal 500Live from the analytics “Verified followers” card.
Verified impressions — 500K / 90 daysOfficial Original Content Rewards count from your eligibility page when available, plus a tracker estimate from Displayed impressions.
